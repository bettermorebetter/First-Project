The Poker Hand Scorer

Our Poker Hand Scorer is a simple tool that lets you draw a five-card hand and instantly see its ranking. Whether you're new to poker and want to learn the hand rankings, or you're just looking for a quick diversion, this tool is a great way to test your luck and learn more about the game. The scoring system is based on standard poker rules, providing a fun and educational experience.

The Cat vs. Dog Classifier

Have you ever wondered if your pet (or even yourself!) has more of a cat-like or dog-like appearance? Our Cat vs. Dog Classifier is a fun experiment in machine learning. Simply upload an image, and our model, powered by Google's Teachable Machine, will analyze it and tell you whether it thinks the image is of a cat or a dog. It's a great demonstration of the power of modern AI, and a fun way to interact with cutting-edge technology.

Please note that this tool is for entertainment purposes only. The model has been trained on a specific dataset and may not be 100% accurate, but it's a fun way to see how machine learning can be used to classify images.
